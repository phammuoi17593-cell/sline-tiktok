Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Sline AI Manager handles personal information in connection with its social media content management services.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features enabled and permissions granted, the application may process account profile information, authorized video metadata, performance metrics, and content submitted by users.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide authorized account connections, organize content, support performance analysis, generate content suggestions, and prepare user-approved publishing actions.

4. AI and Service Providers

Where AI features are enabled, selected content may be processed by AI service providers. The specific providers and categories of data shared must be disclosed by the service operator before those features are activated.

5. Data Storage and Security

The application operator is responsible for implementing appropriate security controls, limiting access to authorized personnel, and protecting account authorization credentials.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Users may request access to or deletion of personal data held by the application operator. Retention periods and deletion procedures will depend on the implemented services and applicable legal requirements.

7. Third-Party Platforms

Connected social media platforms have their own privacy policies and terms. Users should review those policies independently.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the application or applicable laws. The effective date will be updated accordingly.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, please contact the application operator through the contact information published on the application website.
